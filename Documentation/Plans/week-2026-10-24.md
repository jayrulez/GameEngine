foundation.ui. Read against foundation.gui at the user's question, three are closed there: dashed
and dotted borders (`border-style`, StyleApplier.cppm), a keyframe's own timing function (each
segment eased by its first key's, StyleAnimation.cppm), and the faint halo at a thin border's
corners, which the GUI never draws as background and border are one VG box (8cf71d4c4). A
drawable's `var()` lengths have no counterpart in the GUI. The entry is reframed for the GUI and
keeps only the open gap: `drop-shadow()` and the colour filters, both needing a colour pass in VG.
