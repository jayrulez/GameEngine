## Editor.Scene's pages move to their domains (2026-10-18)

From the 0.1 backlog (user 2026-10-16): Editor.Scene held the pages of other domains beside the
scene page, and play in editor too. Each now lives in its domain's editor library, as the audio,
font, texture and shader pages already did, and builds and is tested there:

| Library | Module | What moved | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editor.Animation | `editor.animation` | the animation graph, clip and skeleton pages | Editor.Animation.Tests |
| Editor.Materials | `editor.materials` | the material page | Editor.Materials.Tests |
| Editor.Geometry | `editor.geometry` | the mesh page (static, skinned and model meshes) | Editor.Geometry.Tests |
| Editor.Particles | `editor.particles` | the particle effect page | Editor.Particles.Tests |
| Editor.Render | `editor.render` | the settings profile page | Editor.Render.Tests |
| Editor.Play | `editor.play` | play in editor: the Game tab, its resolutions, IPieInstancePage, the PIE tools, the game-audio settings | Editor.Play.Tests |

**Where play in editor lives** (the item asked for a decision): in Editor.Play, a library of its
own, since it runs a project rather than editing a scene or an asset. It builds on Editor.Scene
(a Game tab captures its viewport as a scene page does) and plugs in through the context's
existing `GamePageFactory`; `RegisterPlayInEditor` sets that and contributes the PIE tools, so
Editor.Scene no longer depends on play in editor and `RegisterSceneEditor` no longer takes the
embedded application. entity_inspect's `pie` reads a running game through a narrower service the
scene module owns, `IGameScenePage` (the tab's id, whether it runs, its scene), which
IPieInstancePage builds on; the editor's MCP tools are unchanged.

Editor.Scene keeps what scenes share: the settings rows (the profile page uses them, so
Editor.Render builds on Editor.Scene as Editor.Play does), the profile model, loading and framing
a project scene, the viewport capture, and the asset thumbnails, which share one preview stage
(the mesh, material, skeleton, model and particle thumbnails stay with it). The other new
libraries need nothing of Editor.Scene. `PrettifyPropertyName`, which the inspector and the
material page share, moved to Editor.App beside `ResourceRefEditor`. The moved pages now import
what they name (the fonts, the settings store) instead of reaching it through `editor.scene`.

Commits: 4b0969394 (animation), 9a8efc245 and 17ec87b84 (the label helper, materials), bf846aa26
(geometry), c7a4d0129 (particles), 9e4a4a767 (render), f23974c26 and 7d755d3b4 (the scene seam,
play in editor), c0933c004 (the particles document). Built and tested on clang and gcc:
Editor.Scene.Tests and every new suite pass, and the editor builds.

## The UI survey's open items (2026-10-18)

From the 0.1 backlog's UI survey (2026-10-14), worked down in the order of what shows soonest for
the least risk (user: "do it"). Every commit carries its tests; UI.Tests and Engine.UI.Tests pass
on clang and gcc, VG.Backend.Tests on Vulkan and WebGPU, and the editor builds.

- **A shadow follows its background's corners** (55992d2bf), the survey's bug: Sky Hopper's cloud
  and HUD cards round their background with `rounded-rect(..., radius=24)` and set no
  `corner-radius`, so they cast square shadows. A drawable now reports its box's corners
  (`OutlineRadii`), and a view's shadow, rounded clip and backdrop blur (ad043edb3) all follow
  `View::OutlineRadii`.
- **Rounded clipping** (6262888ce): a clipping view with rounded corners clips its children to
  them through VG's nested clip paths.
- **Box paint** (f8f70fa6a, b336b8fde): `rounded-rect` takes 1, 2 or 4 border widths and a colour
  per side; `box-shadow` takes a list of up to four, and a transition fades an added one in.
- **Gradients** (3f9b677cb): `linear-gradient()` and `radial-gradient()` in CSS's form, with
  colour stops, angles and `radius=`.
- **Baseline alignment** (8f9fc8fc2): a row's baseline-aligned items line their first lines up.
- **Device-pixel rounding under a scaled canvas** (a34288de0): layout rounds at the scale a box
  is drawn at, so game UI at a reference resolution lands on whole pixels; the hosting is
  unchanged.
- **Motion** (e3b14d971, 2532c1305, f9e272c44): transitions ease by CSS's cubic-bezier curves and
  steps (ease-in at its midpoint is 0.315, not the quadratic's 0.25), sheets take `transform`,
  and `@keyframes` with `animation` play on their own.
- **Colour filters** (4f41bf155, 9cc3c8f14): `filter` takes `grayscale()`, `saturate()` and
  `brightness()` beside `blur()`, applied at the layer's composite.

Also: the rich-text effect test counts each glyph (f6a808940), a gap the Beef port found. A
content-shaped glow, more colour filters, CSS's transform order and origin, border styles, `var()`
in drawables and per-keyframe timing stay in the backlog. Looks to check: Sky Hopper's cards and
their shadows, and anything a theme now rounds, clips or animates.

## Transforms as CSS writes them, animation events, var() in drawables (2026-10-18)

Three of the survey's leftovers the user chose to do now (after "which are worth doing now?"):

- **Transforms in written order** (9f80934ac): a sheet's `transform` applied translate, rotate,
  then scale whatever order it wrote them in, and pivoted only about the view's own origin. It is
  now a list of functions (translate, scale, rotate, skew and their X and Y forms; a translate's
  percent is of the view) composed as CSS composes them into one 2D matrix, `Transform2D` (the GUI
  lab's form), about `transform-origin`. Views draw, hit-test, map and round through that one
  matrix. Transitions go function by function, pad `none` with identities, and otherwise mix the
  decomposed matrices the short way.
- **Animation events** (2d32964c0): a view raises `OnAnimationEnd` and `OnAnimationIteration` with
  the @keyframes' name, through the mutation queue after the frame's tick, and every script handle
  takes `onAnimationEnd` and `onAnimationIteration` (a `UiAnimationHandler` taking the name), so a
  screen can chain a slide-in or remove a faded toast. `game-ui.md` documents them.
- **var() in drawables** (d38ccffa6): `rounded-rect`'s fill and border, `color()` and gradient stops may be
  `var(--name[, fallback])`, resolved against the custom properties of the view being drawn, so one
  rule's background takes each view's accent and a theme switch reaches backgrounds. Lengths in a
  drawable, a side's own colour and state-rounded's derived states stay literal (backlog).
