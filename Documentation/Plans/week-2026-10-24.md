## The editor's icon set and elevation, closed by the editor on the GUI (2026-10-28)

Two items from the 0.1 backlog (the UI survey, 2026-10-14), which the user judged done by the
move to the new editor (Specs/editor-gui.md, X0 to X9, merged to master as 85c7927ab):

- **An icon set for the editor.** The survey counted about 34 editor icons and 10 window glyphs
  against LunarSong's 377, so the toolbars and menus were mostly text. The editor now ships 155
  SVG icons of our own drawing (ruling 3 of the spec): 77 for the interface, in the toolbars,
  menus, tabs and pickers, and 78 type icons, tinted by category in the asset browser, the
  hierarchy and the inspector's headers.
- **The editor theme gives nothing elevation.** RoundedDark put no shadow on what floats. The
  editor's sheet (`Data/Editor/editor.css`) shadows the menus, combo lists, the flag and colour
  popups and the tooltips, the dialogs, the toasts and the drag ghost, from the theme's
  `--shadow-color`; the GUI's default theme shadows windows and toasts as well.

Both left the backlog in 070717ff8.
